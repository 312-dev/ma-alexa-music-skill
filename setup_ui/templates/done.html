Setup complete

The skill is created, the catalog is uploaded and the skill is enabled.

Try it

Say this to any Echo on the same Amazon account:

“Alexa, play Radiohead on {{ state.alias|lower or 'ampere' }}”

Entity resolution can take a few minutes to become active after an upload. If Alexa answers from another music service, check the ingestion state and the skill's enablement on the status page.

Configuration

These can be changed independently and in any order.

  • Status Ingestion state, enablement, and the last request received from Amazon.
  • Alias Check a candidate word against your library before adopting it.
  • Stations How stations are built, and what plays when a queue runs out.
  • Endpoint Re-run the reachability checks after a certificate or hostname change.
Keeping the catalog current

Adding music to your library does not update Alexa. Re-run the upload step to send the changes, or schedule catalog_sync.py, which performs the same upload and then re-enables the skill.
