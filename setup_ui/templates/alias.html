Alias

The alias is the word you say after "on": "Alexa, play Radiohead on ampere". Alexa resolves what you asked for against your uploaded catalog before it decides which provider to route to, so every artist, album, track, playlist and genre in your library competes with the alias word for that slot.

Which means collisions are specific to your library and cannot be listed in advance. Here, "jukebox" was taken by the artist Jukebox The Ghost and the track Juke Box Hero; "gray tunes" resolved to the artist Conan Gray.

One class of failure this page cannot find for you is mishearing. "phono" was heard as "Sonos" every single time, and no textual or soundalike test finds that pair. Say your shortlist out loud to an Echo before committing to one.
