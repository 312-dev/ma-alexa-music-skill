Alias

The word said after "on". Checked against your own library, because that is what it competes with.

The alias is the word you say after "on": "Alexa, play Radiohead on ampere". Alexa resolves what you asked for against your uploaded catalog before it decides which provider to route to, so every artist, album, track, playlist and genre in your library competes with the alias word for that slot.

Collisions therefore depend on your library and cannot be listed in advance. A candidate that matches, or closely resembles, anything you own is likely to be interpreted as a request for that music rather than as a provider name.

This check cannot account for speech recognition. A word may be transcribed as a different one entirely, which no textual or phonetic comparison will predict. Say your shortlist to an Echo before adopting one.
