Alexa resolves what you asked for against your uploaded catalog before it decides which provider to route to. Every artist, album, track, playlist and genre in your library therefore competes with the alias for that slot, so a word that matches something you own will be interpreted as a request for that music instead.

Collisions depend on your library and cannot be listed in advance. The alias page checks a candidate against your own catalog. It cannot predict misrecognition, so say your shortlist to an Echo before adopting one.
