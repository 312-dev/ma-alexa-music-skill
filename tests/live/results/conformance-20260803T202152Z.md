## Cross-run stability

Added after the run, from four full passes on 2026-08-03 (18:44, 19:06, 19:52,
20:21 UTC) plus targeted re-runs of the noisy cells. The JSON alongside this file
is the machine record of the **last** pass only; this section is the part that
one pass cannot show.

The system under test accepts a command, returns success, and intermittently
does nothing. That is a measured property of Amazon's side, not of this suite -
it is why `AmperePlayer._confirm_volume` exists and resends up to three times,
and why a four-speaker group change was observed landing on two of four
speakers on the first send. A cell that passes four runs out of five is
therefore **data**, and is recorded as a rate rather than retried until the
board looks green.

| --- | --- | --- |

| `stop`, all sources | **0/1** as asserted | reaches `idle`, reports `paused` ~5s later. Deterministic once looked for. |
| `volume` on the group | **0/4** | asked 20; the group reports 50 or 0, never 20 |
| `pause` on the group | **~2/3** | the pause is lost and the group returns to `playing` ~10s later |
| `next` / `previous` | **~5/6** | the index moves and is then reverted to the track Alexa is still reporting |
| everything else in the grid | **4/4** | - |

The four rows above the last one are the findings. The rates on `pause` and
`next`/`previous` are what a single run cannot tell you: run 2 failed
`pause-streaming-group` and `pause-radio-group` and passed `pause-subsonic-group`,
and run 3 did the reverse. The source is not the variable; the attempt is.
